📖 How to Play Quiz Quest

1
Answer Multiple-Choice Questions: Each question offers 4 options. Click your answer or press keys A, B, C, D or 1, 2, 3, 4.
2
Scoring & Lives: Correct answers yield +10 Points. You start with 3 Lives. Every mistake costs 1 life.
3
5 Challenging Realms: Progress through escalating difficulty tiers from Level 1 (Easy) to Level 5 (Mastery).
4
Dual-API Intelligence: In Mixed Mode, questions alternate between the Open Trivia DB and Google Gemini AI with instant offline fallback.
